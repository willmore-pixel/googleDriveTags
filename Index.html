Drive Tags

Welcome to Drive Tags 👋

Add tags like vintage or pattern to your Google Drive files, then find them in seconds, on your phone or computer.

Your files and tags are private. Only you can see them, not even the person who shared this app with you. The app can only look at your files. It can't change or delete anything.

Tip: in the Tags tab you can group tags into categories, like Brand or Garment type.
